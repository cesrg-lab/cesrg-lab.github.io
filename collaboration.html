FÜ
Türkiye

Fırat University

Fırat Üniversitesi
Elazığ, Türkiye
International Research Project Design and Development of Intelligent Battery Pack for Range Extension of Electric Scooter

Fırat University is an international research collaborator in the PSF-CRP/TÜBİTAK-IV funded project. The collaboration advances research in active cell balancing, battery management systems, power electronics and intelligent control of lithium-ion battery packs. Joint research focuses on high-fidelity battery and balancing-network modeling, state-of-charge estimation, balancing-current and power-loss analysis, thermal management and robust nonlinear model predictive control. These efforts aim to improve battery utilization, efficiency, reliability, safety and the driving range of electric vehicles.

KFUPM

King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals
Dhahran, Saudi Arabia

KFUPM contributes expertise in advanced energy systems, power electronics, battery technologies and electrical engineering. The collaboration focuses on the development of active cell balancing strategies for battery energy storage systems, aiming to improve cell utilization, state-of-charge uniformity, efficiency, reliability and battery lifetime for small-grid applications.
