About CESRG

Engineering Solutions Through Research

The Control & Energy Systems Research Group (CESRG) at COMSATS University Islamabad focuses on the modeling, analysis, and control of complex dynamical systems, with emphasis on robotics, energy, and electro-mechanical systems.

⚙ Nonlinear & Robust Control 🎯 Optimal & Predictive Control 💻 Data-Driven Control 🔋 Energy Conversion Systems 🧠 State Estimation & Observation 🛡 Fault Tolerant Control
